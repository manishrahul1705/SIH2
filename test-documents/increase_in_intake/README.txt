=====================================================

How to test: choose this application type in ApprovalTrack; answer applicability questions to enable the matching conditional slots; upload the matching PDF by filename; wait for AI analysis; then inspect the extracted suggestions and validation findings.

REQUIRED_* files correspond to unconditional Required (R) entries in the current development checklist. CONDITIONAL_* files correspond to Conditional (C) entries and are only required when the application's answers make that slot applicable. Review-required checklist entries remain subject to the application's answers.
